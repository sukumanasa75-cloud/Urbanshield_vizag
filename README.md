# UrbanShield – Visakhapatnam
> **"Predict Before It Becomes a Problem."**

A smart environmental awareness and predictive web platform engineered specifically for **Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh, India**. Built as a premier college fest technological innovation showcase demonstrating proactive environmental intelligence.

---

## 🌟 Core Concept: MONITOR → PREDICT → WARN → ACT

Traditional environmental tools only display static historical readings or current levels when damage has already occurred. **UrbanShield** bridges this gap by forecasting multi-vector environmental trajectories before thresholds become critical:

1. **MONITOR:** Track current air quality (AQI, PM2.5, PM10, NO2), traffic delays, municipal solid waste generation, and edible food surplus across 25 Visakhapatnam sectors.
2. **PREDICT:** Multi-horizon predictive projections (1h, 3h, 6h, 12h, 24h) evaluating localized risk trajectory curves.
3. **WARN:** Automated early warning triggers alerting citizens and municipal stakeholders to imminent pollution or traffic spikes.
4. **ACT:** Area-tailored mitigation recommendations and an interactive **"What-If Impact Simulator"** modeling the tangible benefits of immediate intervention.

## 🚀 Key Modules & Features

- **Interactive Leaflet Risk Map:** Centered on Visakhapatnam `[17.728, 83.285]` with color-coded risk markers (Low, Moderate, High, Critical) and pulsating radar auras for critical hotspots.
- **Area Analysis:** Radial gauge scores, 5-vector diagnostic cards, and the **"Explain Risk"** attribution breakdown highlighting primary vs secondary risk drivers.
- **Predictions Dashboard:** Current vs Predicted comparisons across 1h, 3h, 6h, 12h, and 24h intervals with interactive Recharts trend graphs.
- **What-If Impact Simulator ("WHAT IF WE ACT NOW?"):** Interactive sliders for Traffic, Air Emissions, Solid Waste, and Food Waste with real-time recalculation of the future risk score.
- **Compare Two Areas:** Objective side-by-side benchmark matrix and comparative bar charts without subjective bias.
- **Actionable Recommendations:** Dynamic mitigation playbooks customized to the active area's highest risk drivers.
- **Early Warning System:** Live predictive alert feed with suggested interventions.
- **Generate Area Report:** Print-ready and downloadable environmental dossier.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- **Frontend Framework:** React 18 + Vite
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS (Modern Glassmorphism & Coastal Dark Theme)
- **Mapping:** Leaflet + OpenStreetMap Carto Tiles
- **Visualizations:** Recharts (Multi-line curves, Bar charts, Donut charts)
- **Icons:** Lucide React
- **Data Source:** Calibrated simulated predictive models for the college fest demonstration (*clearly marked as Simulated Demo Data*).
